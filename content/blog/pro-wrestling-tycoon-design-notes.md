Pro Wrestling Tycoon started with a simple question: what does it feel like to *run* a promotion, not just watch the matches?

## The core loop

We wanted a loop you could repeat in a few minutes but still care about over a season:

1. **Scout and sign** wrestlers with distinct strengths
2. **Book a card** — match types, rivalries, and stakes
3. **Run the show** and read the fallout — ratings, injuries, crowd reactions
4. **Reinvest** in training, marketing, or bigger venues

Every screen in the game should answer: *what decision am I making next?*

## UI over spectacle

Wrestling games often lean on flashy presentation. We're aiming for clarity first—schedules, contracts, and match results should be scannable at a glance. Personality shows up in wrestler bios, show names, and the little flavor text—not in clutter on the management screens.

## What's next

We're iterating on booking depth (stables, long-running angles) and tightening the feedback loop after each show. If you're playing early builds, we'd love to hear what decisions feel meaningful versus busywork.

More notes as we go.
