This is the CharmByte studio blog: a place for notes that don't fit on a landing page.

We'll use it for:

- **Release notes** and patch highlights for our games
- **Design notes** on systems we're building and why
- **Process** — how we prototype, playtest, and polish

Right now we're a small team focused on making games that feel tight and readable. Our first title is [Pro Wrestling Tycoon](http://www.pwtycoon.com)—a management sim about building and booking your own wrestling promotion.

More posts will land here as we ship. Thanks for reading.
